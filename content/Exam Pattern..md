The RBI exam is conducted in 3 phases
### Phase 1
The Phase 1 exam is qualifying in nature and each section has its own cutoff along with the total cutoff of the exam.

| Section Name          | Total Questions | Maximum Marks | Weightage | Sectional Duration |
| --------------------- | --------------- | ------------- | --------- | ------------------ |
| General Awareness     | 80              | 80            | 40%       | 25 Minutes         |
| Reasoning Ability     | 60              | 60            | 30%       | 45 Minutes         |
| English Language      | 30              | 30            | 15%       | 25 Minutes         |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30              | 30            | 15%       | 25 Minutes         |
| **Total**             | **200**         | **200**       | **100%**  | **120 Minutes**    |

### Phase 2 
| Section Name | Subject Domain                       | Assessment Format               | Marks       | Time Allocation             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------- |
| Paper 1      | [[Economic and Social Issues (ESI)]] | 50% Objective + 50% Descriptive | 100 (50+50) | 120 Mins (30 Obj + 90 Desc) |
| Paper 2      | English (Writing Skills)             | 100% Descriptive (Typed)        | 100         | 90 Minutes                  |
| Paper 3      | Finance & Management (FM)            | 50% Objective + 50% Descriptive | 100 (50+50) | 120 Mins (30 Obj + 90 Desc) |
### [[Economic and Social Issues (ESI)]]
#### Pattern
The paper of **100 marks** is divided into:
1. Objective Part
2. Descriptive Part
#### Objective Part

|           | No of Questions   | Total  |
| --------- | ----------------- | ------ |
|           | 10 (1 marks each) | 10     |
|           | 20 (2 marks each) | 40     |
| **Total** | **30** questions  | **50** |
|           |                   |        |

Total time for objective section is **30 minutes**. 
Majority questions are from CA, PIB, international reports, government schemes, current events. 
#### Descriptive Part
Total 6 questions are provided and 4 are to be answered.

|       | No of questions   | Total | Word Limit |
| ----- | ----------------- | ----- | ---------- |
|       | 2 (10 marks each) | 20    | 400 - 420  |
|       | 2 (20 marks each) | 30    | 600 - 620  |
| Total | 4 questions       | 50    |            |

Total time for objective section is **90 minutes**.
